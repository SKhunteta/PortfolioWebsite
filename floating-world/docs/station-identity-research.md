# Station identity research

Source notes behind `src/data/station-identity.json` — what each Link
station actually looks like, researched July 2026 from Sound Transit, the
STart public-art program, Wikipedia, and architecture/transit press. Each
accent hex is drawn from the station's built materials or signature
artwork, not from a palette.

## 1 Line — downtown & central

**Westlake** `#b4593a` · underground (DSTT, 1990). Art Deco elements in pale
stone; 1,264 handmade terra-cotta tiles carved as leaves, vines and flowers
(lead artist Jack Mackie), platform murals by Roger Shimomura, Fay Jones,
Gene Gentry McMahon. Accent: the carved terra-cotta.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westlake_station_(Sound_Transit)) ·
[DSTT art](https://bytesandboxes.org/2021/12/29/public-art-transit-tunnel/)

**Symphony** `#d92b12` · underground. High-tech register: linear stainless
lighting, glass mezzanine rails, granite accents; Robert Teeple's 60-ft LED
"Electric Lascaux" and Bill Bell's "Northern Lights" light sticks. Accent:
the red LED glyphs.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symphony_station_(Sound_Transit))

**Pioneer Square** `#c08573` · underground. "Pioneers and Timelessness":
tall vaulted hall, gray granite at the south end (King County Courthouse),
pink granite north (Public Safety Building); Ericson & Ziegler's artifact
clocks, Garth Edwards' cut-steel grillwork. Accent: the pink granite.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pioneer_Square_station_(Sound_Transit))

**Int'l Dist/Chinatown** `#c0281c` · at-grade (open trench beside Union
Station). Red-trimmed plaza, Sonya Ishii's nine folded stainless origami
panels, zodiac pavers in Coast Salish style. Accent: the plaza red.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_District/Chinatown_station) ·
[Origami wall](https://www.codaworx.com/projects/origami-wall-seattle-metro/)

**Stadium** `#69be28` · at-grade. 400-ft island platform, 30 ft wide for
game-day surges, between Lumen Field and T-Mobile Park. Accent: Seahawks
action green.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadium_station_(Sound_Transit))

**SODO** `#96502a` · at-grade, on the old busway. Michael Davis's "Made in
USA" — a gateway of overscale rusted workbench tools — and the SODO Track
mural corridor. Accent: the rusting steel.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SODO_station_(Sound_Transit)) ·
[SODO Track](https://sodotrack.com/about/)

**Beacon Hill** `#0047ab` · underground, 160 ft down, elevator-only — the
deepest in Seattle. Dan Corson's "Space Forms" (glowing nudibranch-like
sculptures under a cobalt ceiling) and "Portals". Accent: the cobalt.
[Seattle Times](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/beacon-hills-light-rail-station-filled-with-colorful-art/)

**Mount Baker** `#9fc131` · elevated. Boxwood design; Guy Kemper's
mouth-blown glass "Seattle Sunrise" (bright green and yellow) and Sheila
Klein's "Sky Within" chandeliers. Accent: the sunrise glass green.
[Kemper Studio](https://www.kemperstudio.com/seattle-sound-transit-mt-baker)

## 1 Line — Rainier Valley & south

**Columbia City** `#8a6b3f` · at-grade, MLK median. Victoria Fuller's
35-ft bronze "Global Garden Shovel" cast from world plants; Gale McCall's
bronze baskets and magnifying glasses. Accent: the bronze.
[Public Art Archive](https://publicartarchive.org/art/Global-Garden-Shovel/3302c7ee)

**Othello** `#ef8a38` · at-grade, MLK median. Roger Shimomura's "Rainier
Valley Haiku" stacked totem (sandal, Creamsicle, rice bowl…); Augusta
Asberry's cut-steel dancers. Accent: Creamsicle orange.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Othello_station)

**Rainier Beach** `#b3bcc2` · at-grade island platform. Darlene
Nguyen-Ely's brushed-aluminum "Dragonfly"; Buster Simpson's "Parable"
(cabled pear halves). Accent: brushed aluminum.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rainier_Beach_station)

**Tukwila Int'l Blvd** `#93b8b1` · elevated, 51 ft, hilltop. David Hewitt's
white winged V-roof ("the slope and wing of airplanes"), glass walls to Mt.
Baker and the Sea-Tac tower; Tad Savinar's giant raindrop. Accent:
green-tinted glass and pale steel.
[Hewitt](https://hewittseattle.com/project/tukwila-international-boulevard-station/)

**SeaTac/Airport** `#7c8fa0` · elevated, 60 ft. Column-free steel trusses;
Werner Klotz's wind-driven "Flying Sails"; Christian Moeller's "Restless".
Accent: stainless/truss gray-blue.
[Hewitt](https://hewittseattle.com/project/seatac-airport-station/)

**Angle Lake** `#5b8fbe` · elevated. Brooks + Scarpa's undulating screen of
7,500+ blue-anodized aluminum panels (LEED Gold); Haddad|Drugan's "Cloud"
of 6,000 shimmering acrylic discs. Accent: the blue-anodized canopy.
[Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2018/01/03/brooks-scarpa-drapes-wavy-metal-screen-over-seattle-angle-lake-transit-station-plaza/)

## 1 Line — Federal Way extension (2025)

**Kent Des Moines** `#c98e4a` · elevated center platform. Steel canopy with
glass clerestory; RE:site Studio's "Cambium" tree-ring art glass bridging S
236th. Accent: warm cambium amber.
[Sound Transit](https://www.soundtransit.org/system-expansion/kent-des-moines-station)

**Star Lake** `#d94f70` · elevated embankment beside I-5. Tory & Eroyn
Franklin's "Gather": 324 ceramic-frit glass panels of bright flowers and
edible plants. Accent: the floral pink-red.
[Kent Reporter](https://www.kentreporter.com/news/sound-transit-to-feature-glass-art-in-kent-at-star-lake-station/)

**Federal Way Downtown** `#63c5d8` · elevated terminus, twin three-story
glass headhouses. Catherine Widgery's "Prismatic" dichroic fins; Donald
Lipski's "Leafy Wader". Accent: dichroic cyan-teal.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Way_Downtown_station)

## 1 Line — north

**Capitol Hill** `#d0342c` · underground, ~65 ft (Hewitt). Mike Ross's
"Jet Kiss" — two pink-and-yellow Navy A-4 jets over the platform; Ellen
Forney's murals on bright-red porcelain-enamel panels; green wall on
terracotta-painted trellis. Accent: the Forney red. (The oft-repeated
"weathering steel facade" claim did not verify.)
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Hill_station)

**Univ of Washington** `#38a3b5` · underground, ~100 ft (LMN). Glass entry
pavilion by Husky Stadium; Leo Saul Berk's "Subterraneum" — ~6,000 backlit
panels of geologic glyphs "glowing a deep aqua blue". Accent: that aqua.
[UW News](https://www.washington.edu/news/2016/03/25/geology-and-art-connect-at-uw-light-rail-station/)

**U District** `#f18a21` · underground, 85 ft (LMN). Black granite entries;
white corrugated vault; orange (north) and blue (south) wayfinding light
tubes; Lead Pencil Studio's "Fragment Brooklyn". Accent: the orange tube.
[LMN](https://lmnarchitects.com/project/u-district-station-sound-transit)

**Roosevelt** `#cfa83a` · underground, ~90 ft box (Hewitt). Brick-and-glass
headhouses, restored 1947 neon radio sign; R & R Studios' gold stepped
pyramid "Building Blocks"; Luca Buvoli's "Mo-Mo-Motion". Accent: the gold.
[Sound Transit](https://www.soundtransit.org/blog/platform/roosevelt-history-lives-new-light-rail-station)

**Northgate** `#4aa495` · elevated, 25–45 ft (Hewitt). Floating cantilevered
roof; Mary Ann Peters' "Darner's Prism" — dragonfly imagery across 100 ft
of painted glass. Accent: the blue-green darner.
[KUOW](https://www.kuow.org/stories/behind-the-art-in-north-seattle-s-new-light-rail-stations)

## 1 Line — Lynnwood extension (2024)

**Shoreline South/148th** `#2f6bce` · elevated (LMN). Blue elevator towers,
yellow kiosks; Buster Simpson's "Flow" — bright-blue stormwater cisterns.
Accent: Flow blue.
[LMN](https://lmnarchitects.com/project/shoreline-south-148th-station-sound-transit)

**Shoreline North/185th** `#a6d422` · at-grade in a retained-cut trench.
Mary Lucking's "Octavia's Garden": forged neon-green ferns and vines
honoring Octavia E. Butler. Accent: fern green.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoreline_North/185th_station)

**Mountlake Terrace** `#4a7c59` · elevated astride 236th St SW, treetop
height. Kipp Kobayashi's "Re-Forestation" lenticular forest panels.
Accent: forest green.
[HNTB](https://www.hntb.com/projects/mountlake-terrace-station-lynnwood-link-extension/)

**Lynnwood City Center** `#f0a070` · elevated terminus (LMN). Peach canopy,
blue towers (city colors); Claudia Fitch's 30-ft neon "City Hummingbird".
Accent: the peach.
[LMN](https://lmnarchitects.com/project/lynnwood-city-center-station-sound-transit)

## 2 Line — Eastside

**Judkins Park** `#a3752c` · at-grade in the I-90 trench. Hank Willis
Thomas's "Crosstown Traffic": 50-ft bronze-anodized halftone murals of Jimi
Hendrix; Barbara Earl Thomas's windscreen glass. Accent: the bronze
halftone.
[Sound Transit](https://www.soundtransit.org/blog/platform/fresh-pics-honoring-jimi-hendrix-judkins-park-station)

**Mercer Island** `#56789b` · at-grade in the former I-90 express lanes
(Hewitt). Glass "saddlebag" volumes; Beliz Brother's suspended oars and
boat. Accent: lake-glass blue.
[Hewitt](https://hewittseattle.com/project/mercer-island-station/)

**South Bellevue** `#7a8b2e` · elevated, 35.5 ft, beside Mercer Slough.
Vicki Scuri's "Photo Synthesis": 900 ft of hexagon panels shifting gold to
green with the slough's seasons. Accent: the gold-green.
[Vicki Scuri](https://www.vickiscuri.com/project-bellevue.html)

**East Main** `#ddd6c6` · at-grade beside Surrey Downs; low butterfly-roof
canopies in mid-century register; Celeste Cooning's lacework art fence.
Accent: pale mid-century cream.
[LMN](https://lmnarchitects.com/project/east-main-station-sound-transit)

**Bellevue Downtown** `#c26d4b` · at-grade at the downtown tunnel mouth
(LMN). Terracotta panels echoing City Hall, tree-inspired glass canopy,
20-ft cyclorama lightbox. Accent: the terracotta.
[LMN](https://lmnarchitects.com/project/bellevue-downtown-station-sound-transit)

**Wilburton** `#d0342c` · elevated over I-405. Bold red entrance canopies;
Phillip K. Smith III's 40-ft mirrored "Four Corners Extruded". Accent: the
canopy red.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilburton_station_(Sound_Transit))

**Spring District** `#9e4a3a` · at-grade in an open trench below the
brick-heavy TOD. Louie Gong's cut-metal dragon and phoenix span the
platform walls. Accent: district brick.
[Sound Transit](https://www.soundtransit.org/blog/platform/art-our-stations-swirling-dragon-phoenix-greet-new-2-line-riders)

**BelRed** `#7c93a8` · at-grade, Spring Blvd median. Patrick Marold's
460-ft hand-crafted stainless railing throwing waterlike shadows. Accent:
water-steel blue-gray.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BelRed_station)

**Overlake Village** `#3fae5a` · at-grade beside SR 520. Leo Saul Berk's
"Verdant" wraps the pedestrian bridge in louvers painted like green
phosphor CRT forest. Accent: phosphor green.
[Hewitt](https://hewittseattle.com/project/overlake-village-station/)

**Redmond Technology** `#d7dde3` · at-grade center platform amid
Microsoft's campus, under the 1,100-ft white fabric-canopied bridge
(AECOM). Accent: canopy white.
[Seattle Times](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/transportation/new-walk-bike-bridge-debugs-access-to-microsoft-and-light-rail/)

**Marymoor Village** `#6a994e` · at-grade island platform at the park's
edge; Nova Jiang's "Air Grove" seed-airships; Yegizaw Michael's mosaic.
Accent: park green.
[Sound Transit](https://www.soundtransit.org/blog/platform/how-art-reflects-community-downtown-redmond-marymoor-village)

**Downtown Redmond** `#1f8a99` · elevated terminus above Cleveland St.
Twenty-five 12×6-ft flat-glass mosaic murals by six artists (Tieton
Mosaic). Accent: mosaic teal.
[Tieton Mosaic](https://www.tietonmosaic.com/project/redmond-station)
